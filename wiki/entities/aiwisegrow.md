# Entity — AIWise Grow

**Kind:** company (AI automation / implementation services)
**Sources:** [company-profile](../sources/aiwisegrow-company-profile.md) · [gtm-technical-requirements](../sources/aiwisegrow-gtm-technical-requirements.md)

A young, founder-led business that builds **supervised ("human-in-the-loop") AI automation systems in the client's own accounts** — the client owns the accounts, code, and documentation; risky actions pause for review. This is a custom-implementation model, explicitly *not* a rented multi-tenant SaaS. Founder **Queenal Vrishni** (Westford, MA); `aiwisegrow.com` domain registered 2025-06-20; associated operator **Rohit Jain**. *(All from [company-profile]; treated as Confirmed there.)*

## Market positioning (unresolved)
The company tells two overlapping stories: a **fitness/wellness** niche (LinkedIn, the "Wellness with Purpose" podcast, older site, and its internal plan) vs. **broad small-business agent automation** (current website). The profile recommends settling this before finalising the service catalogue. *(from [company-profile])*

## Delivery model & service lines
The internal **GTM spec confirms five service lines**: Lead-Management/Follow-Up Automation, Cold Email, Voice AI, Website & SEO, and Dashboards/Reporting — delivered via per-client branded sub-accounts, with a phased rollout (pre-revenue → first close → scaling) and quality gates before handoff. Pricing uses "Steve Farina's complexity scale." *(from [gtm-technical-requirements]; this corroborates the "internal requirements plan" the [profile] only inferred.)*
