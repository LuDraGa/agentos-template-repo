## What this source is

A role spec for an **Entrepreneur-in-Residence / AI Builder** to found BoldCap's engineering arm and make the VC firm "AI-native, from first signal to final decision." A zero-to-one builder seat, not an internal-IT role.

## Key content (faithful)

- **What you'd build:**
  - *Deal sourcing* — agents that discover/enrich/connect/rank fragmented signals, human investor in the loop.
  - *Talent Radar* — a living map of exceptional builders (technical talent, founder trajectories, team formation, networks).
  - *Market intelligence* — turning messy public/private signals into evolving market maps and theses.
  - *The firm's memory* — retrieval/research/knowledge systems that make every memo and decision more reusable.
  - *Precise rails* — data pipelines, identity resolution, permissions, evaluation, observability, feedback loops, reliable cloud infra.
- **How you'd work:** gather requirements → roadmap; prototype in days; build evals/feedback loops *alongside* the product; automate where human judgement adds no edge; "fast but noisy is not useful."
- **Toolkit:** Python + modern AI app dev; *harness engineering* (giving models context, tools, memory, evaluation, guardrails); LLMs/retrieval/agents + judgement to know when not to use them; cloud/production basics; product sense. Hiring bias toward *evidence of building* over pedigree.
