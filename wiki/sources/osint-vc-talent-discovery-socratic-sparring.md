## Key content (faithful)

**Domain (OSINT for VC):**
- OSINT is a concept/methodology, not a database, library, or tool; as a field it has standardized frameworks, intelligence cycles, and verification practices.
- Viable for discovering/screening early-stage, research-backed founders (DeepTech, AI, biotech, hard tech) where LinkedIn is thin. Sources: Google Scholar, GitHub/Hugging Face, patents, grants, research labs/universities, technical communities. Screening signals: peer/expert engagement, quality of public technical work, OSS collaboration, academic credibility, affiliations, project history, risk indicators.
- Constraints established: strong public signals are what make it viable; those signals are **non-standard for very-early research founders** and must be discovered experimentally; **OSINT alone is insufficient** (needs CRM, scoring, ML/NLP, pipelines, heuristics); prefer **buy commodity data infra + build the differentiated signal/scoring/workflow**.

**Method (named in the reflection):** Socratic questioning + first-principles decomposition + binary (T/F) constraint framing + metacognition + active hypothesis testing. The agent should act as a *Socratic sparring partner* that surfaces blind spots while requiring the human to commit to an answer first. Includes a reusable prompt (extracted into the pattern page).
