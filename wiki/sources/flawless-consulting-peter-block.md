## What this source is

A classic practitioner guide on **getting your expertise used** — exercising influence when you do not have direct control. Its through-line is that technique is not enough: effective consulting rests on *authenticity* and *partnership* with the client.

## Structure (from the table of contents — reliable provenance even though the body is unread)

The consulting arc the book teaches:
- **Contracting** (chs. 4–7): elements and ground rules, the contracting meeting, its "agonies," internal vs external consultants.
- **Resistance** (chs. 8–9): the faces of resistance and three steps for handling it.
- **Discovery** (chs. 10–13): from diagnosis to discovery, whole-system discovery, discovering "gifts/capacities/possibilities" (positive deviance), "getting the picture."
- **Feedback & action** (chs. 14–15): preparing feedback, managing the meeting for action.
- **Implementation / engagement** (chs. 16–17): choosing *engagement over installation*.
- **Extensions** (chs. 18–19): the consultative stance applied to teaching/learning ("the question is more important than the answer").
