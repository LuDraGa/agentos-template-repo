## What this source is

AIWise Grow's internal spec for its go-to-market delivery infrastructure: the five service lines, the functional components to deliver each, and a phased rollout. Everything ships under the AIWise Grow brand (no fulfilment-partner names in client-facing output).

## Key content (faithful)

- **Five service lines:** (1) Lead Management & Follow-Up Automation, (2) Cold Email systems (dedicated sending domain, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, ≥14-day warming), (3) Voice AI (inbound/outbound agents with human-escalation path), (4) Website & SEO (conversion-first, client-owned accounts, AIWise Grow retains partner access only), (5) Dashboards & Reporting (one branded view-only scoreboard per client).
- **Per-client branded sub-accounts**; each service line has functional components, build requirements, and day-numbered delivery milestones.
- **Shared infrastructure:** Agency CRM hub, client onboarding, internal comms, file storage/SOPs, contract execution, pricing calculator (uses "Steve Farina's complexity scale"), an internal "Human AI OS," branded proposal delivery.
- **Phased rollout:** Phase 1 pre-revenue (infra readiness, SOPs) → Phase 2 first close (clients 1–2; capture 30/60-day results) → Phase 3 scaling (clients 3–5; tech VA executes builds, founder focuses on discovery/strategy).
- **Quality gates** before every handoff (live-lead test, reviewed test call, staging approval, real dashboard data, signed contract on file).
