# AIWise Grow — company profile and public-footprint review

**Research date:** 16 September 2026  
**Purpose:** establish what is publicly known about AIWise Grow before deciding what should be productised, customised, or sold as a managed service.

This is a research snapshot, not legal or financial due diligence. It separates public facts from reasonable inferences and unverified claims.

## The short version

AIWise Grow is a young, founder-led automation business based publicly in Westford, Massachusetts. Its public footprint is real but early: a recently rebuilt website, an active LinkedIn presence, and an active wellness podcast on YouTube.

The company is telling two slightly different stories. Its LinkedIn activity, podcast, older website material, and internal plan are strongly tied to fitness and wellness studios. Its current website is broader. It sells “teams of agents” for outbound, social content, SEO, and internal operations to founders and small teams in several industries.

That difference matters. The narrow story gives AIWise Grow a believable entry point and access to a specific community. The broad story creates a larger market, but makes the business look like one of many general automation agencies. The market position should be settled before the service architecture and sales material are finalised.

No named AIWise Grow client, attributable case study, or independently verifiable delivery result was found in the public sources reviewed. This does not mean there are no clients or delivered projects. It means the proof is private, unpublished, or not yet created.

## What is confirmed

- **Brand and domain:** AI Wise Grow / AIWiseGrow, using [aiwisegrow.com](https://www.aiwisegrow.com/). The domain was registered on 20 June 2025, so this is a relatively young public brand. The registry record is available through [Verisign RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/AIWISEGROW.COM).
- **Founder:** Queenal Vrishni is named as founder on the website and is the visible voice of the business on LinkedIn and the podcast.
- **Public location:** the [LinkedIn company page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aiwisegrow) lists Westford, Massachusetts. LinkedIn describes the company as privately held, in IT Services and IT Consulting, with a self-reported size of 2–10 people.
- **Another associated operator:** [Rohit Jain](https://www.linkedin.com/in/getrohitjain) lists AIWiseGrow in his experience and has an engineering and delivery background. His exact role and employment status are not publicly clear.
- **Owned media:** AIWise Grow operates the [Wellness with Purpose](https://www.youtube.com/@WellnessWithPurposeOfficial) YouTube channel and podcast, hosted by Queenal.
- **Public contact path:** the website provides `contact@aiwisegrow.com` and a strategy-call booking link. No public price list was found.

The precise legal entity that signs AIWise Grow contracts is not yet confirmed. A Massachusetts registry search did not find an entity under the exact AIWise Grow name. Queenal is publicly associated with Arborum Ventures LLC, but no source reviewed proves that it owns or operates the AIWise Grow brand.

## What AIWise Grow currently sells

The [current website](https://www.aiwisegrow.com/) presents the company as an implementation partner that builds supervised agent systems in four areas:

1. **GTM and outbound:** list building, enrichment, sequences, and reply triage.
2. **Social:** content repurposing, calendars, approvals, and publishing.
3. **SEO:** topic clustering, briefs, internal linking, and technical audits.
4. **Internal operations:** onboarding, reporting, data hygiene, and hand-offs.

The stated customer is a founder or team of fewer than 20 people, especially in operations, RevOps, marketing, or growth.

The public delivery promise is important. AIWise Grow says systems are built in the client’s own accounts; the client retains the accounts, code, specifications, and documentation; risky actions stop for human review; and work is fixed-scope and fixed-timeline. The site explicitly says the result is **not a dashboard rented from AIWise Grow**.

That is a custom implementation model. It can support recurring managed services, but it is not the same promise as a conventional multi-tenant SaaS product. If AIWise Grow later offers a shared platform, the two offers will need clear names, ownership rules, pricing, and boundaries.

## The positioning shift

The older and still-indexed message is more specific: growth systems for boutique fitness and wellness studios. It focuses on lead response, trial-to-member conversion, retention, re-engagement, churn, and revenue visibility. Queenal’s public content and the podcast continue to reinforce this niche.

The website was materially refreshed in September 2026. Its [sitemap](https://www.aiwisegrow.com/sitemap.xml) marks the current pages as updated on 14 September and the home page now speaks to small businesses more generally.

So three versions of the company currently coexist:

| Source | Message |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn, podcast, older pages | Automation and growth for fitness and wellness operators |
| Current website | Agent teams for GTM, social, SEO, and operations across small businesses |
| Internal requirements plan | Five service lines for studios, with custom builds, sub-accounts, and possible white-label delivery |

This looks like active repositioning, not necessarily confusion. But it creates a decision for the scope: is fitness and wellness the company’s defining market, its first market, or merely one customer segment?

## Presence and reach

### Website and search

Exact-name search works: someone searching for “AIWise Grow” can find the official site and LinkedIn page. Non-branded discovery is weak. In a sample of searches for AI automation and lead-follow-up services for fitness studios, AIWise Grow did not appear prominently.

The current website is clearer and more restrained than older indexed material, but it is small. Its public sitemap contains only home, contact, learn, and podcast pages. The Learn section contains summaries rather than substantial linked articles. Retired pages and changed content are still visible in search, so a buyer can encounter the older and newer stories in one session.

### LinkedIn

The company page is active and showed 117 followers at the time of review. Queenal’s personal account has the greater reach and acts as the main distribution channel. Recent themes include founder bottlenecks, lead response, nurture, churn, studio retention, wellness stories, and podcast promotion.

Follower counts are only point-in-time platform snapshots. They indicate activity, not commercial traction.

### Wellness with Purpose

The podcast is AIWise Grow’s strongest public relationship asset. On the research date, its YouTube channel showed **98 subscribers, 81 videos, and 14,391 views**. The channel began in February 2026 and was publishing regularly in September.

Guests and featured organisations include Spirit Stone Wellness, Music4Life, Be Well Beautiful People, Love Before ALL, and Desk to Dusk. Some guests have publicly acknowledged their interviews; for example, [Spirit Stone Wellness](https://spiritstonewellness.com/resources) links to its conversation with Queenal.

These are confirmed media relationships. They must not be described as customers, commercial partners, or product users without direct confirmation.

No separately attributable AIWise Grow profile on Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok was verified in the searches performed. Such profiles may exist but be unindexed or use a different name.

## Clients and proof of delivery

No publicly verifiable named client was found on the website, LinkedIn, review sites, or in independent search results. The public presence contains no client logo wall, named testimonial, implementation case study, or customer reference.

An older growth page contained striking figures, including revenue, conversion, churn, and administrative-time improvements. It did not name the studio, state the baseline period, show a data source, or explain the method. Those figures should be treated as **unverified marketing claims**, not evidence of AIWise Grow results.

There is credible adjacent operating experience. A [2024 Kidokinetics franchise disclosure document](https://storage.googleapis.com/franchiseindx_fdds/Fitness/Kidokinetics_2024_bo67fz22) lists Queenal Vrishni and Rohit Jain through Pioneer Adventures Inc. and indicates ownership of three franchises as of 31 December 2023. This supports first-hand familiarity with a fitness operation. It is not evidence that Kidokinetics was an AIWise Grow client or that an AIWise Grow system was deployed there.

The most accurate public description today is therefore:

> AIWise Grow is building automation systems for small businesses, with a strong founder network and operating context in fitness and wellness. Its public client evidence is not yet mature.

## What the footprint suggests

**Strongest assets**

- A founder with visible access to wellness operators.
- First-hand fitness-franchise operating context.
- An active niche interview and content engine.
- A clear, buyer-friendly promise around human oversight and client ownership.
- A recent website that explains implementation more clearly than many early-stage automation firms.

**Material gaps**

- No public proof of a completed client implementation.
- No confirmed legal contracting entity under the brand name.
- No clear public team structure or delivery capacity.
- Two competing market positions.
- Older numerical claims without a visible evidence trail.
- No public security, privacy, service-level, or support material suitable for systems that access CRM, inbox, calendar, or customer data.
- Little non-branded search visibility and no meaningful independent review footprint.

## Implications for the technical and commercial scope

1. **Treat fitness and wellness as the initial proof market unless leadership explicitly chooses otherwise.** The company already has language, relationships, and operating context there.
2. **Do not assume a mature client base or a standardised platform.** Ask to inspect actual projects, automations, accounts, code, and support history.
3. **Preserve the current ownership promise.** The website commits to client-owned accounts, code, and documentation. A shared SaaS lane can still exist, but it must be presented as a separate choice rather than a hidden change in delivery model.
4. **Make the first strong case study part of delivery.** Record the starting numbers, workflow, launch date, human review steps, 30/60/90-day results, and permission to publish.
5. **Use the podcast as both research and distribution.** It can reveal recurring studio problems and create warm pilot opportunities without turning guests into implied customers.
6. **Resolve the market sentence before designing the full service catalogue.** A useful working version is: “AIWise Grow builds supervised growth and operations systems for fitness and wellness businesses, then adapts the proven components for other small service businesses.”

## Information to request privately

Before finalising the scope, request:

- the legal entity and any DBA under which contracts are signed;
- a full list of paid projects, pilots, internal builds, and unpaid trials;
- the deliverables, tools, dates, and present status of each project;
- permission status for naming each client and publishing results;
- supporting data for every performance claim;
- Queenal’s, Rohit’s, and any contractor’s delivery and support roles;
- the production systems currently operating, their volume, and incident history;
- whether the Kidokinetics operation was used as a test bed;
- which podcast relationships are guests, referral partners, pilot prospects, or customers;
- whether client ownership of accounts, code, and documentation applies to every planned service line.

## Evidence standard used in this brief

- **Confirmed:** directly supported by a first-party record or a credible independent source.
- **Claimed:** stated by AIWise Grow but not independently demonstrated.
- **Inferred:** a reasonable reading of several facts, clearly labelled as such.
- **Unknown:** not established in the public sources reviewed.

Public sources can miss private projects, unpublished results, login-only pages, and informal relationships. “Not found” in this brief means **not publicly verified**, not “does not exist.”
