# Grill Me

Read [SCHEMA](../../../SCHEMA.md) first. This is decision clarification, not a learning quiz or permission to execute.

## Procedure

1. Inspect available context first; start at the wiki index when brain context is relevant and follow only relevant links. Do not ask questions already answered.
2. Identify the highest-leverage unresolved decision, contradiction, or assumption.
3. Ask **one decision at a time**, explain why it matters, and offer a **recommended answer** with rationale and alternatives when useful. Label the recommendation as advice, not an agreed decision.
4. Tighten vague answers into testable constraints. Surface contradictions and get a clear choice instead of silently resolving them.
5. Stop once the next action can proceed responsibly, or mark the blocker if the user cannot decide. Resume from agreed decisions rather than restarting an interview.

## Closeout

Finish with **Agreed decisions**, **Unresolved risks**, and **Next action**.

## Boundaries

- **Side effects:** none by default; the interview is transient. Any later promotion follows SCHEMA, not a blanket requirement to ask the user to ingest.
- **Approval:** recommendations are not consent. Do not execute consequential actions or record unresolved positions as commitments.
